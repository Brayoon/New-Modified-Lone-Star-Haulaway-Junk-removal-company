Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost?

Every job gets a free estimate based on volume, item type and access. We confirm the price before work begins.

How soon can you pick up?

Often within the same week, and sometimes next day. Call or text Peter at +1 (302) 250‑0862.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. If items are accessible and we have a confirmed price, we can work while you’re away.

What can’t you take?

Hazardous materials such as paint, chemicals, asbestos and biohazards need special handling. Ask us and we’ll point you to the right option.

Do you donate or recycle?

Yes. Usable items go to donation partners and recyclables are sorted whenever possible.

Can you help with hoarding situations?

Yes, with privacy and respect. We work at your pace and can coordinate with family or professionals.

How can I pay?

Debit or credit card, mobile money and other methods. See the payment section below.
